## What to test

Share a plan directly from its details. Existing connections open the plan; new
friends can connect and then view it, including after signing in. New plans shared
with connections now send an alert that opens that plan. Private plans notify
only invited friends; edits do not repeat the alert. Plan alerts can be turned off
under Your Village → Requests → notifications. Allow iOS notifications to receive
alerts. Please try both receiving a friend's plan and sharing one yourself.
