## What to test

Share a plan from its detail screen. Connected friends open that exact plan;
new friends can connect and then view it. Links survive email sign-in and profile
setup. For invited-only plans, accepting the creator’s link invites just that
person to the plan. Please test sharing, opening a link from Messages, connecting
with a new friend, and returning after sign-in. Use this build for the complete
plan-link flow. Send feedback through TestFlight or beta@withvillage.app.
